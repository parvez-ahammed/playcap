# Responsible use

playcap automates something you could do by hand: open a page in your own
browser, press play, and record your screen until the video ends. It is a
time-shifting DVR for video you are entitled to keep: your own hosted videos,
webinars and classes you are enrolled in where the provider allows offline
copies, accessibility copies for poor connections, and QA monitoring of a web
player.

## Protected video records black, by design

Players that use hardware-backed DRM usually render black to screen capture.
playcap treats that as the site's decision. It detects the black frames, tells
you, and skips the item. It will not try to get around it, and requests to make
it do so will be closed.

## What playcap does not do, and will not do

- It does not decrypt DRM, extract media streams, intercept license or key
  exchanges, or download media files. The only thing it captures is what OBS
  sees on your screen.
- It does not log in for you, copy cookies, tokens or sessions from other
  browser profiles, or share accounts.
- It does not bypass device limits, concurrency limits, paywalls, geo-blocks
  or other access controls.
- It ships no adapters or recipes for paid streaming or course platforms, and
  the project will neither accept nor link to third-party ones.

Pull requests that add any of these will be closed. Issues asking how to make
playcap work on a specific paid platform will be closed and locked with a link
to this page.

## Your responsibilities

- **Entitlement.** Only record content you have the right to watch, using
  your own account.
- **Terms of service.** Many sites forbid recording in their terms. Read them.
  Whether a recording is allowed depends on the site, the content and your
  country's law, and that is for you to check.
- **Personal use.** Keep recordings for your own study, reference or
  accessibility needs.
- **No redistribution.** Do not upload, share, stream or sell recordings of
  content you do not own.
- **Other people.** If a recording includes other participants (a live class,
  a meeting), get the consent your local law requires.

## The law differs by country

This page is not legal advice. Private-copying rules vary a lot. The United
Kingdom, for example, currently has no private-copying exception at all, and
several EU countries read copy-protection rules broadly. If you are unsure
whether you may keep a copy of something, ask the provider or don't record it.

## Rights holders

If you believe playcap is being promoted or used to infringe your rights,
open an issue with the **Rights holder concern** template, or report it
privately through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/parvez-ahammed/playcap/security/advisories/new).
Concerns are answered before anything else.
