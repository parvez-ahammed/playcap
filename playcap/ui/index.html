Recording quality

How OBS compresses the video while it records. Quality modes keep the picture the same everywhere and let the size follow it: a still slide costs almost nothing, motion costs more. Those files are final. Fixed bitrate gives a predictable size per hour but wastes space on still pictures; "Re-compress library" can shrink those later.
