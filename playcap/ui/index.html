        Recipes

        A recipe tells playcap which element on a page is the video player, what to click to start it, and which links on an index page lead to the videos. Without one, playcap picks the largest video on the page.

        1. Point at the page

        A bar appears at the top of the playcap browser window. Choose Video player and click the video; optionally Play button; on an index page, Item links and click two or three of the video links. Then press Use my picks. You can pick on an index page, press Use my picks, open a video page in the same tab and pick again.

        2. Check the recipe

        Index page optional: build the queue from a page of links
