Screen recorder

Which program records the screen. OBS is the default: mature, and it records the computer's sound on its own. "ffmpeg only" needs no OBS, but it can record sound only from a sound device that carries what the speakers play (Stereo Mix, virtual-audio-capturer or a virtual audio cable); without one it records the picture only. Either way playcap checks that the capture is not black.

Recording quality

How OBS compresses the video while it records. Quality modes keep the picture the same everywhere and let the size follow it: a still slide costs almost nothing, motion costs more. Those files are final. Fixed bitrate gives a predictable size per hour but wastes space on still pictures; "Re-compress library" can shrink those later.
